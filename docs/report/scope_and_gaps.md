## Decisions and deviations

### D1 — Databricks only; no Snowflake comparison

**Checked 2026-09-29.** `SHOW AVAILABLE LISTINGS` on our Snowflake trial (4,222
listings) has three FHIR listings, all by Kipi.bi. The closest, "Kipi Health Data
Utils - FHIR Synthetic Data" (`GZSTZ4CLE1N7D`), is a different product: Kipi's own
generator, "10K static data records" per entity, delivered as an installable app.
The Databricks listing is Synthea bundles published by Databricks.

Same standard, different data, so a platform comparison would compare two
datasets, not two platforms. **Decision (user, 2026-09-29): build on Databricks only.**

No listing named "FHIR R4 Read with SQL" exists on either marketplace; it is the
name of the notebook inside the Databricks listing.

### D2 — The data is synthetic

Synthea patients are generated, not real. Good for building and testing the
pipeline with zero privacy risk. Its numbers must **never** be presented as
real-world health facts: every report and chart says "synthetic".

## Known from the listing's notebook (to confirm on our own run)

- Medications appear in `medicationCodeableConcept`, not the standard `medication`
  field — the notebook's authors call this non-compliant.
- Only institutional (837I) and pharmacy claims; no professional claims (837P).
- Resources are linked by ids inside each bundle (e.g. a Claim's `patient`
  points to a Patient `id`); a broken link is a quarantine case.
